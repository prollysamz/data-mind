# DataMind Datasets Reference & Provenance

This document records the official provenance, source URLs, licenses, row counts, and verification notes for all primary and secondary datasets supported in DataMind.

---

## 1. Primary Demo Dataset: SaaS Enterprise Operations (SYNTHETIC)
- **Status:** Synthetic demonstration data (1,450 sales rows, 5 products, 100 support tickets). All support ticket outages correlate directly with Novaship revenue drops to provide a deterministic demonstration. Not to be cited as real-world commercial evidence.
- **Generator:** `examples/primary/build_demo_data.py` (Fixed PRNG seed = 42 for reproducible row generation).
- **Format:** DuckDB (`examples/primary/demo.duckdb`) & Snowflake (`DATAMIND.PUBLIC.SALES`, `PRODUCTS`, `SUPPORT_TICKETS`).
- **Tables:**
  - `SALES`: 1,450 rows (order_id, line_no, order_date, order_month, product_id, product_name, quantity, amount).
  - `PRODUCTS`: 5 rows (product_id, name, category, monthly_price_usd, release_date).
  - `SUPPORT_TICKETS`: 100 customer support tickets indexed in SQLite FTS5 (`.datamind/fts.sqlite`).
- **License:** Apache-2.0, like the rest of this repository (the data is generated by `examples/primary/build_demo_data.py`).

---

## 2. Secondary Real-World Dataset: UCI Online Retail
- **Official Source:** UC Irvine Machine Learning Repository
- **Dataset Title:** Online Retail
- **Official URL:** [https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/352/online+retail](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/352/online+retail)
- **Donor:** Dr. Daqing Chen, Director of Public Analytics Group, School of Engineering, London South Bank University, London SE1 0AA, UK (`chend@lsbu.ac.uk`).
- **License:** [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
- **Total Records:** 541,909 transactions
- **Timeframe:** 01/12/2010 to 09/12/2011
- **File:** `examples/secondary/online_retail/Online_Retail.csv` (~43.9 MB)
- **Verification Invariant:** Loaded directly through the user-uploaded dataset pipeline with zero source file edits.

## 3. Test Fixtures (SYNTHETIC, For Automated Unit & Upload Testing Only)
- **`tests/fixtures/chinook_tracks.csv`**: Relational music catalog sample (30 rows), used for unit testing upload column mapping and typing.
- **`tests/fixtures/customer_feedback_messy.csv`**: Hand-curated test fixture containing BOM, semicolon delimiters, duplicate headers, and PII patterns to test upload profiler defenses.
- **Integrity Notice**: Neither test fixture is cited as evidence of accuracy or real-world benchmark performance.

---

## 4. Primary Real Benchmark Dataset: Olist Brazilian E-Commerce (WP-F1)
- **Source:** Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist (99k orders, 99k reviews with Portuguese customer text).
- **Setup:** Downloaded directly by the human from Kaggle into `data/olist/` (git-ignored) and loaded into Snowflake and DuckDB for authentic multi-table evaluation in WP-F1.
- **License:** CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (to be verified upon download). Not bundled in the repository.
